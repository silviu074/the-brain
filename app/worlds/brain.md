## What it is
- A visual way to follow what AI does on your computer. Rules, skills, agents, plugins, MCP servers and folders orbit the core and light up while Claude works.
- HISTORY replays your past prompts step by step. LIVE follows the chat that is running right now.
- HEAT and COST show what you really use and what every message carries.

## Where the data comes from
- Your .claude folder and the project you used most recently. Press SYNC after you change your setup.
- Everything stays on this computer. The server only listens on 127.0.0.1.

## This file
- worlds/brain.md. Edit it to keep your own notes here.

## A hub for your apps
- Each named planet is one app or project. Add yours in app/worlds.js and give it a notes file here (worlds/<id>.md).
- Suggested habit: one handoff per project. See docs/HANDOFFS.md in the repo.
