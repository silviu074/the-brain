## What it is
- A live 3D map of your Claude Code setup: rules, skills, agents, plugins, MCP servers and folders orbit the core.
- HISTORY replays your past prompts step by step. LIVE follows the chat that is running right now.
- HEAT and COST show what you really use and what every message carries.

## Where the data comes from
- Your .claude folder and the project you used most recently. Press SYNC after you change your setup.
- Everything stays on this computer. The server only listens on 127.0.0.1.
